The Westray Craft Association's knitting circle met on Thursday as usual: eleven of us, two dogs and more tea than was wise.

Ingrid Flett showed the yoke she is designing from the colours of the cliffs at Noup Head, and promised the chart for next week. We also agreed to swap patterns at the Christmas fair.
